Merle, The Kind Incompetent Wizard

Overview and introduction

Overview

Merle, The Kind Incompetent Wizard is a 3D platformer inspired by A Hat in Time, with visual references from Luigi’s Mansion 3. The game takes place across three levels where the player controls a small wizard, explores the world, jumps between platforms, and unlocks abilities through collectible hats.

One of the main gameplay abilities is a fireball spell, which becomes available after collecting a specific hat. My work on the project focused on visual effects, lighting, cubemaps, and procedural shader-based motion for pickups and levitating objects.

My contributions

My role in the project focused mainly on tech art and visual presentation.

Fireball VFX

I created the fireball effect used by the player once the ability is unlocked. This included the full effect chain from the moment the spell is prepared to the final impact.

  • Charge-up effect — built to make the spell feel readable and satisfying before release
  • Throw effect — the fireball projectile itself and the visual energy around it
  • Impact effect — the hit effect when the fireball collides with the environment or a target

Lighting and cubemaps

I worked on the lighting for all three levels and also created cubemaps for them. A large part of this work was about supporting the mood of each level and making the environments feel more polished and atmospheric.

Because the game takes visual inspiration from Luigi’s Mansion 3, the lighting was important for giving the world a more stylized and playful feel while still keeping it readable for gameplay.

Pickup and levitation animation

I also worked on procedural vertex animation for certain objects in the game. This included pickup items that gently float and rotate, as well as levitating objects in the environment.

These animations were created to give the world more life and to make important gameplay objects feel more noticeable and magical.

Game direction

The game was designed as a charming and playful 3D platformer, drawing inspiration from A Hat in Time in its movement, tone, and collectible-driven progression, while using Luigi’s Mansion 3 as a visual reference for atmosphere and presentation.

This combination shaped both the gameplay and the visual work. The effects, lighting, and floating object animations were all created to support a world that feels whimsical, readable, and full of personality.

Challenges

  • One challenge was making the fireball effects feel powerful and readable while still fitting the stylized tone of the game.
  • Another challenge was finding lighting setups that worked across three different levels while still giving each level its own mood and identity.
  • For the floating pickups and levitating objects, it was important to create motion that felt magical and alive without becoming too distracting.

If I had more time

  • I would continue polishing the fireball effects to push the charge-up, throw, and impact stages even further.
  • I would like to refine the lighting more across all three levels to strengthen the mood and visual identity of each area.
  • I would also expand the procedural object animations with more variation and secondary motion.
