Spite Oathbound

Overview and introduction

Overview

Spite Oathbound is a top-down action game inspired by Diablo 3, but with a Viking-themed visual direction. The player takes control of a shield maiden and fights through different levels filled with enemies and larger boss encounters.

My work on the project focused on technical art and gameplay-related visual effects. I created a shader-based highlight effect for enemies, a scrolling projectile trail mesh, and boss-related mesh and particle effects.

My contributions

My role in the project focused on shaders and combat-related visual effects.

Enemy hover and special enemy shader

I created a shader effect for enemies that reacts when the player hovers over them with the mouse. This made enemies more readable and helped give clearer visual feedback during gameplay.

In the same shader, I also handled special enemies. These enemies were always glowing, and when hovered they reacted with a different color than normal enemies. This helped separate them visually and communicate that they were more important or dangerous.

Projectile trail mesh

I created a projectile trail mesh that used a scrolling texture to give projectiles a stronger sense of movement and direction. The goal was to make attacks feel more dynamic and readable from the top-down camera view.

Since the game is inspired by action RPGs like Diablo 3, readable combat effects were important for making abilities feel responsive and satisfying.

Boss beam and mouth VFX

For the large giant boss, I created a beam mesh effect as well as a smaller particle effect at the mouth. These effects were made to support the boss attack visually and make the ability feel more threatening.

The beam mesh gave the boss attack a stronger shape and direction, while the particle effect at the mouth added a buildup point and helped connect the attack to the character.

Visual direction

The project takes inspiration from the structure and feel of Diablo 3, but shifts the presentation into a Viking-themed setting. Because of that, the effects needed to feel readable and powerful from a top-down view while still fitting the darker fantasy tone of the world.

My work was mainly about supporting combat readability and enemy clarity. The hover shader, projectile trail, and boss effects were all created to help the player read threats and interactions more clearly during gameplay.

Challenges

  • One challenge was making the enemy hover effect feel clear without becoming too strong or distracting.
  • Another challenge was making special enemies feel visually distinct while still keeping them consistent with the rest of the game.
  • For the projectile trail and boss beam, it was important to make the direction of the effects readable from the top-down camera angle.

If I had more time

  • I would like to continue polishing the enemy shader with even more variation between normal and special enemies.
  • I would refine the projectile trail further to give it more depth and stronger motion.
  • I would also expand the boss effects with more buildup, impact, and secondary VFX to make the encounter feel even stronger.
